Tutorial / Contribute

Submit an Asset to OpenConstruction

01Prepare the Required Information

Before beginning, gather the official asset title, DOI when available, license, original public resource URL, author or contributor information, and a short description of the asset.

02Open the Contribute Page

From the public OpenConstruction website, open the Contribute page.

03Select Contribute

Select Contribute in the main navigation to begin the contribution process.

04Open the Submission Workspace

Under Propose Resources, select Submit resource. This opens the OpenConstruction submission workspace.

05Sign In Securely

Sign in using a trusted identity provider or secure email link. OpenConstruction does not store passwords.

06Enter Contact Information

Begin the submission with your current name and email address so reviewers can contact you if clarification is needed.

07Enter Your Affiliation

Add your organization or institutional affiliation in the provided field.

08Select the Asset Type and Title

Choose whether the asset is a dataset, model, open educational resource, or workflow, then enter its official title.

09Add the DOI

Enter the asset's DOI when one is available. A DOI is optional but useful for verification.

10Choose the License

Select the license that accurately governs reuse and sharing. Confirm it against the original public source before submitting.

11Write a Short Summary

Add a concise note that gives reviewers useful context about the asset, its scope, or its intended use.

12Confirm Authorship and Permissions

Under Authorship and credit, select the option that accurately describes your relationship to the asset and your permission to submit it.

13Choose Community Recognition

Complete the required permission statement and indicate whether contributor names may appear on the public OpenConstruction Community page.

14List the Contributors

Add the names of contributors who should receive credit for the asset or submission.

15Review the Submission Checklist

Before submitting, confirm that the contact information is current, metadata and license are accurate, no private or sensitive information is included, and the record may be published by OpenConstruction.

16Confirm Your Contact Information

Select the confirmation stating that your contact information is current.

17Confirm the License Information

Select the confirmation stating that the license information is accurate.

18Confirm No Private Information Is Included

Confirm that the submission contains no private or sensitive information.

19Agree to Publication

Confirm that the submitted record may be published in OpenConstruction after review.

20Submit the Resource

When every field and confirmation is correct, select Submit resource.

21Understand the Review Process

Your contribution will be reviewed before it is added to the OpenConstruction catalog. Review may include checks for metadata quality, attribution, licensing, and public-source verification.

You are now ready to prepare a complete resource record and submit it for OpenConstruction review.
