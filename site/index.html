Overview

Open infrastructure for reusable AEC resources

OpenConstruction brings AEC datasets, models, workflows, and educational resources into one open catalog.

  • Standardized metadata and validation for consistent discovery
  • Benchmarks and linked context for research, educational use, and deployment
  • Open contribution, review, and governance for long-term stewardship

Help improve the platform through Contribute or Community.

2022.10
A Rough Idea Takes Shape
Sparked at ECCV’22, the idea of lifelong learning to a larger vision - sharing and reusing AEC knowledge for community.

Project Team Established
Two years later, the idea matured into action. A core team was assembled to turn the concept into a real platform for OpenConstruction.

Our work is described in a synthesis of open construction datasets , shared to support and inspire ongoing community efforts.

Policy & Feedback

Questions, corrections, or removal requests

OpenConstruction indexes publicly available metadata and links to original sources. We do not host datasets, models, code, or proprietary materials, and inclusion does not imply endorsement or warranty.

For corrections or removal requests, contact support@openconstruction.org. For product feedback or feature ideas, create a suggestion or join the discussion board.
