About the Platform

Open infrastructure for reusable AEC digital assets

Built for researchers, educators, and practitioners in architecture, engineering, and construction.

Standardized Metadata

Standardized metadata and validation for consistent discovery.

Benchmarks & Context

Benchmarks and context for research, teaching, and deployment decisions.
