Guides & Toolkits

Reusable materials for better construction data releases.

Use these toolkits to move from good intentions to publishable artifacts: checklists, templates, workshop materials, and instructor adoption assets.

Dataset publishing checklist

Minimum fields, documentation, file structure, license, DOI, sensitive data review, benchmark readiness, and OpenConstruction submission steps.

  • Required metadata
  • Release readiness review
  • Reuse and citation notes

README and dataset card templates

Templates tailored to construction imagery, video, BIM, point clouds, geospatial layers, safety observations, equipment logs, and sensor streams.

  • Collection protocol
  • Annotation definitions
  • Known limitations

Instructor adoption kit

Syllabus language, a one-week assignment, discussion prompts, assessment rubric, and a lightweight certificate completion report for faculty.

  • Graduate course module
  • Thesis orientation activity
  • Workshop handout

Turn workshops into acquisition channels.

Run practical events such as "How to Publish a High-Quality Open Construction Dataset." Participants complete pre-work, improve their own data during the session, and finish the Academy afterward.

  • BeforeComplete two or three micro-modules.
  • DuringApply the checklist to a real dataset.
  • AfterFinish assessment and submit or improve a resource.
