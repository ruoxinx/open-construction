Tutorial / Your Workspace

Organize Your Research with Workspace

01Welcome to OpenConstruction Workspace

Workspace turns one-time browsing into an organized research workflow. Because it stores saved resources and comparison lists for later use, Workspace requires you to sign in.

02Sign In Securely

Open the public sign-in page. Use a trusted identity provider such as GitHub, Google, or ORCID, or request a secure email link. OpenConstruction does not store or manage passwords.

03Browse and Save Relevant Resources

Browse the public catalogs and save resources that look relevant. For example, you might save a dataset or model to revisit later in your Workspace.

04Review Saved Items

Open Workspace to review the resources you saved. Your Personal Library keeps those candidates together and supports comparison across multiple items.

05Open the Comparison Tool

Select the resources you want to evaluate together, then choose Compare to open the comparison workflow.

06Compare Selected Candidates

Use the comparison view to examine selected items side by side. Focus on the metadata and evidence most relevant to your research decision.

07Export Selected Information

Use the export options to save selected resource information outside Workspace. Exported records can support notes, review, or collaboration beyond the platform.

08Organize Evidence for Decisions

Workspace helps you move from discovering resources to organizing evidence and making informed decisions: sign in, save, organize, compare, and export.

You are now ready to turn resource discovery into an organized and reusable research workflow.
