Top Concepts

Stable domain anchors such as Person, PPE, Material, and Activity. These should be few, durable, and standards-aligned.

Taxonomy Classes

Intermediate groupings such as Crane or Wall that organize reusable families without collapsing distinct meanings.

Leaf Concepts

Operational concepts such as Tower Crane, Hard Hat, and Asphalt that link most directly to dataset labels and tasks.

Standards Alignment

Structure follows Brick-style ontology governance, with future alignment to IFC and other AEC standards where semantics truly match.
