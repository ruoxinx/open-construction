# Code of Conduct

## Our Commitment

OpenConstruction is committed to providing a respectful, inclusive, and
collaborative environment for everyone participating in the project and
community.

We expect contributors, maintainers, reviewers, and community members to:

- Be respectful and constructive in discussions, reviews, and feedback
- Welcome people with different backgrounds, viewpoints, and experience levels
- Focus on evidence, clarity, and shared learning
- Respect intellectual property, attribution, privacy, and research ethics

## Unacceptable Behavior

The following behavior is not acceptable in project spaces, online discussions,
or community events related to OpenConstruction:

- Harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, or intimidation
- Abusive, insulting, or inflammatory language
- Deliberate misrepresentation of authorship, affiliation, or ownership
- Sharing confidential, private, or sensitive information without permission
- Knowingly submitting content that violates copyright, license terms, consent,
  or applicable law

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to:

- GitHub issues, pull requests, discussions, and reviews
- Community calls, workshops, and project events
- Email or other communications conducted in project contexts

## Reporting

If you experience or witness behavior that violates this Code of Conduct,
contact the maintainers at `support@openconstruction.org`.

Please include:

- A description of the incident
- Links or screenshots when available
- Any relevant context that may help the maintainers review the report

Reports will be handled respectfully and reviewed as promptly as possible.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers are responsible for interpreting and enforcing this Code of
Conduct. They may take appropriate action in response to unacceptable behavior,
including warnings, content removal, temporary restrictions, or removal from
community spaces.

## Attribution

This Code of Conduct was written for the OpenConstruction project and informed
by common open-source community standards.
